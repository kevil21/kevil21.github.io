## Why it exists

Enterprise AI agents run inside customer infrastructure and emit traces the customer never looks at until something goes wrong. A360 is Kyndryl's platform for watching how those fleets actually behave: executive dashboards, KPI drill-downs, embedded Kibana, agent trust scores, LLM FinOps and a conversational interface over the telemetry. Every tenant sees only its own data.

## The pipeline

Traces arrive as OTLP from the customer's own agents. Postgres stages the raw spans before normalisation. Azure Data Explorer is the source of truth: three fact tables, four dimensions and thirty-one materialized views. A sync service fans those views into per-tenant Elasticsearch indices every ten minutes. A FastAPI gateway in front of Elasticsearch, Kibana, Cosmos DB, LiteLLM and the insight engine validates every request against JWKS. The Next.js 16 frontend, built on React 19 and Kyndryl's Shidoka design system, renders eleven KPI tiles, ten Kibana pages, a chat surface and the agent-trust view.

## What Kevil owned

The résumé states it plainly: lead developer on A360, owner of the ADX → Elasticsearch sync service (31 materialized views, per-tenant indices), the synthetic telemetry generator, and significant portions of the Next.js frontend. The synthetic generator is what lets the platform be demonstrated and tested without customer data: it produces realistic agent traces, dependency telemetry and infrastructure signals for several tenants on a six-hour timer, and backfills about seventy-two hours of data in about fifteen seconds.

## The decisions that matter

The sync service is built to be re-run. Hourly views sync with a seventy-minute overlap and daily views with a twenty-five-hour overlap, and every document carries a SHA-256 identifier derived from its natural key, so overlapping windows upsert instead of duplicating. Tenants are provisioned by a boarding function that creates the tenant's ADX database, Cosmos containers and Elasticsearch index templates, then registers it. And the platform observes itself: A360 emits its own traces into A360 under its own tenant.
